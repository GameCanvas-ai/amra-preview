# Castela surface materials

These freely available textures are from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com), released under [CC0 1.0](https://polyhaven.com/license). Downloaded 13 September 2026. No paid or proprietary game art is used.

- [Cobblestone Floor 03](https://polyhaven.com/a/cobblestone_floor_03) — Rob Tuytel. Used for cobbles.
- [Clay Roof Tiles](https://polyhaven.com/a/clay_roof_tiles) — Amal Kumar. Used for roof.
- [Medieval Blocks 03](https://polyhaven.com/a/medieval_blocks_03) — Rob Tuytel. Used for masonry.
- [White Plaster Rough 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/white_plaster_rough_01) — Rob Tuytel. Used for plaster.
- [Brown Mud Dry](https://polyhaven.com/a/brown_mud_dry) — Rob Tuytel. Used for mud.
- [Wood Planks Dirt](https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_planks_dirt) — Rob Tuytel. Used for wood.

Original 1K JPEG color, OpenGL normal and roughness maps are bundled unchanged. Amra applies UV tiling, surface tint and scene lighting in its renderer. `sources.json` retains official download URLs, sizes and MD5 checksums. Reimport with `python3 scripts/import-city-materials.py`.

The existing Quaternius CC0 village modules, furnishings, characters and foliage source remain credited in [../CREDITS.md](../CREDITS.md).
