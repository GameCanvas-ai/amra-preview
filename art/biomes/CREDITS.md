# Biome study materials

Sand, snow and sandstone: CC0 assets from Poly Haven, downloaded unchanged at 1K. This importer is powered by Poly Haven.

- [sand_01](https://polyhaven.com/a/sand_01) — authors: Rob Tuytel
- [snow_02](https://polyhaven.com/a/snow_02) — authors: Rob Tuytel
- [sandstone_blocks_04](https://polyhaven.com/a/sandstone_blocks_04) — authors: Rob Tuytel

See `sources.json` for exact download URLs, sizes and MD5 checksums. Reproduce with `python3 scripts/import-biome-materials.py`.

The study also reuses the existing credited forest and Castela materials and Quaternius leaf alpha. Geometry, regional layouts and lighting are authored for Amra. These scenes are proposals, not canonical maps.
