# Ataluk playable proof

Geometry and layout authored for Amra in Blender. Not generated from or projected onto the earlier AI illustration.

Surface photographs reuse the credited Poly Haven CC0 sets in `../../castela/CREDITS.md` and `../../forest/CREDITS.md`: mud, wood, forest floor, bark and rock. Earthen plaster, reeds and plant geometry are authored procedural materials and meshes. The Blender script defines the exact set in use.

Architecture is a proposed Ataluk earthen farm compound, revised following the user's requirement for African influences. Construction reference: UNESCO's Royal Court of Tiébélé, https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1713. No website images are included, and no sacred ornament or royal site plan is copied. The design is not asserted to represent all African architecture or all of Alkebu.

The playable character uses the existing separately credited character and animation assets. Its clothing is a proof placeholder, not completed regional costume.
