# Alkebu study sources

`amra-world-map.jpeg` is the unchanged Amra world map supplied in this project (`docs/lore/amra_world_map.jpeg`), 4096×2048. It is displayed with an SVG viewport and a study-anchor overlay. No biome boundaries are claimed by the overlay. Amra map/lore is project material, not covered by third-party CC0 licenses.

Scene materials reuse the credited forest, Castela and biome surface sets. See `../biomes/CREDITS.md`, `../forest/CREDITS.md` and `../castela/CREDITS.md`. The dry sand is `sand_01`, Rob Tuytel, Poly Haven, CC0, imported with the other biome surfaces and recorded in `../biomes/sources.json`.

Geometry and local scene layouts are Amra art proposals. Full source distinctions and placement limits: `FIELD_NOTES.md`.
