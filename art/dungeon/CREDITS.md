Flare dungeon art, Copyright ©2010–2013 Clint Bellanger and contributors. CC BY-SA 3.0 or later. Hythlodaeus: sarcophagus mesh (CC BY 3.0). TiZiana: dungeon stone textures (CC BY 3.0). Full notices: [UPSTREAM-CREDITS.txt](UPSTREAM-CREDITS.txt) and [LICENSE.txt](LICENSE.txt).

Source: https://github.com/flareteam/flare-game/tree/af6eee6d339ac98011864bfe89da837fe7769c28
Original file: `mods/fantasycore/images/tilesets/tileset_dungeon.png`.

The atlas is unchanged. Amra selects and positions tiles, tints them, animates flame opacity, and adds warm light overlays. Rendered adaptations are CC BY-SA 3.0 or later. The map, interaction integration and renderer were authored for Amra; no Flare engine code is used. Generic source statues and canine tomb carvings do not establish new Amra heraldry.

The creature atlas and packed animation definitions come from `mods/fantasycore/images/enemies/skeleton.png` and `mods/fantasycore/animations/enemies/skeleton.txt` at the same revision. Misha contributed the skeleton sword concept (CC BY 3.0); see the complete upstream credits. Amra uses run, stance, swing and cast frames in eight directions, with different tint and scale for the guardian, sentinel and warden. These are visual adaptations of the existing encounters, not a new canonical species claim.
