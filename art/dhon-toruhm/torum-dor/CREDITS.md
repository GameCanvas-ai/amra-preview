# torum-dor

Original Amra image generated with built-in image_gen from user-approved concepts A/C/D/E/F, then edited to remove painted people. Characters are separate runtime models; their existing credits apply. Fixed-camera painted background with manually authored collision and silhouette depth; not a complete 3D reconstruction. Source files: browser/scripts/dhon_surfaces.py and prepare-dhon.py. Local layouts are proposals grounded in the Dhon Toruhm gazetteer.
