# Modeled Dhon Toruhm winter scene

Original Blender geometry authored for Amra by scripts/render-dhon-winter.py and scripts/dhon_winter.py. Approved concept stills guide the visual treatment; they are not projected onto scenery. Scenery, depth and collision come from the modeled scene.

Shared CC0 rock and bark material sources: Poly Haven Rock Face (Greg Zaal, Dario Barresi) and Bark Brown 02 (Rob Tuytel), credited in ../../forest/CREDITS.md. Snow, ice, stone tint, needle color, cloth and metal are authored procedural materials. No new external asset downloads.

Source: docs/lore/8_gazetteer/dhon_toruhm.md. Torum Keep 466–476; Torum-dor 478–488; White Ice Mountains 562–570; forests 474 and 558. Local site layouts are original interpretations. The complete concept-to-geometry production record is in browser/docs/art/DHON_TORUHM_PRODUCTION.md.

Reproduce with AMRA_REGION=dhon-toruhm and AMRA_BIOME=torum-keep-geometry using Blender and scripts/render-dhon-winter.py, then scripts/prepare-ataluk.py with the same biome name. Register coordinates with scripts/register-dhon-geometry.py.
